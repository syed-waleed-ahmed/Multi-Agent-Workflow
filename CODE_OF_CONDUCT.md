# Code of Conduct

Campaign Forge is a small open-source project. Everyone who takes part in it, in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space, is expected to help keep it welcoming and useful.

This code is based on the principles of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.

## Our standards

Expected:

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Give and accept constructive feedback, and credit other people's work.
- Keep discussions on topic, and explain your reasoning.
- Help newcomers find their way.

Not acceptable:

- harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language;
- sexualised language or imagery;
- personal attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption;
- publishing anyone's private information (including API keys or other credentials) without their permission;
- any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in every project space. It also applies when someone represents the project in public, for example in a talk or a post.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer, [Syed Waleed Ahmed](https://github.com/syed-waleed-ahmed):

- **Private report:** open an issue titled "Conduct contact request" with no details, and you will get a private channel.
- **Security problem:** follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) instead.

Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer reviews every report and may take any action that fits: a private warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban from the project's spaces.
